Our Story

More Than Coffee.☕️

AURA COFFEE began as a single roaster and a single room. A decade later, it's still that — a place to slow down, meet a friend, open a laptop, or simply sit with something well made. We roast in small batches, pour with intention, and treat the pause in your day as worth doing properly.
